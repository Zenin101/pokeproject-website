Five Psychic Pokémon

1. Abra

Abra is known for its ability to teleport using its psychic powers.

2. Drowzee

Drowzee uses psychic abilities and is known for its connection to dreams.

3. Slowpoke

Slowpoke is a relaxed Pokémon with both Water and Psychic abilities.

4. Mr. Mime

Mr. Mime uses psychic powers to create invisible barriers.

5. Mewtwo

Mewtwo is a powerful Psychic type Pokémon created through genetic experiments.
