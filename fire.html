Five Fire Pokémon

1. Charmander

Charmander is a small Fire type Pokémon with a flame burning on its tail.

2. Vulpix

Vulpix is known for its beautiful tails and ability to produce fire.

3. Growlithe

Growlithe is a friendly Fire type Pokémon known for its loyalty and courage.

4. Ponyta

Ponyta is a horse-like Pokémon with a mane and tail made of flames.

5. Magmar

Magmar lives in hot environments and attacks using powerful fire moves.
