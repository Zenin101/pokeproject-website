What is Pokémon?

Welcome to the World of Pokémon

Pokémon is a popular franchise that includes video games, trading card games, TV shows, movies and more. It was created by Satoshi Tajiri and Ken Sugimori in 1996. The franchise has become a global phenomenon, captivating fans of all ages with its unique creatures and exciting adventures.

Explore the Pokémon World

There are many different types of Pokémon, each with special abilities, appearances and characteristics. Some are powerful, some are cute, and others have unique skills.
