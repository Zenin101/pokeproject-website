Five Water Pokémon

1. Squirtle

Squirtle is a small turtle Pokémon that uses water attacks from its mouth.

2. Psyduck

Psyduck is a Water type Pokémon that sometimes releases powerful psychic energy.

3. Poliwag

Poliwag is a tadpole Pokémon that swims using its strong tail.

4. Goldeen

Goldeen is a fish Pokémon that swims gracefully through rivers and lakes.

5. Lapras

Lapras is a gentle Pokémon that carries people across large bodies of water.
