Five Grass Pokémon

1. Bulbasaur

Bulbasaur has a plant bulb on its back that grows as it develops.

2. Oddish

Oddish is a small Pokémon that absorbs nutrients from the ground.

3. Bellsprout

Bellsprout has a plant-like body and can use its vines to attack.

4. Tangela

Tangela is covered with blue vines that hide most of its body.

5. Chikorita

Chikorita is a gentle Grass type Pokémon with a leaf growing from its head.
