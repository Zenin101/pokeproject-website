Five Flying Pokémon

1. Pidgey

Pidgey is a small bird Pokémon that uses its wings to create gusts of wind.

2. Spearow

Spearow is a fast bird Pokémon that flies around searching for food.

3. Zubat

Zubat is a bat Pokémon that uses sound waves to navigate in darkness.

4. Doduo

Doduo is a two-headed bird Pokémon that is known for running quickly.

5. Swablu

Swablu is a small bird Pokémon with soft cloud-like wings.
