Five Electric Pokémon

1. Pikachu

Pikachu stores electricity in its cheeks and releases powerful electric attacks.

2. Raichu

Raichu is the evolved form of Pikachu and can produce strong electrical attacks.

3. Magnemite

Magnemite floats using electromagnetic energy and attacks with electricity.

4. Voltorb

Voltorb looks like a Poké Ball and is known for its electrical energy.

5. Jolteon

Jolteon is an Electric type Pokémon that can create electricity through its fur.
