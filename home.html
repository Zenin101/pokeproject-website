Popular Pokémon

Pokémons come in different shapes, sizes and types. Each Pokémon has special abilities that make it unique. Here are some popular Pokémon.

Pikachu

Pikachu is an electric type Pokémon. It is famous for its yellow body, red cheeks and electric attacks. Pikachu is one of the most recognized Pokémon.

Charizard

Charizard is a fire and flying type Pokémon. It looks like a dragon and can breathe powerful fire. It is known for its strength and flying abilities.
